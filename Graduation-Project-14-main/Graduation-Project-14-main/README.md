Marketing Campaign Management System

Graduation Project – 2027

This repository contains the graduation project developed by Fourth-Year students (Class of 2027) at the Higher Institute of Advanced Management Sciences and Computers in Beheira (BHI47).

Project Information

* Project Title: Marketing Campaign Management System
* Group Number: 14
* Academic Year: 2026–2027
* Supervisor: Dr. Mohamed Hegazy
* Institution: Higher Institute of Advanced Management Sciences and Computers in Beheira (BHI47)

Project Description

A web-based system designed to facilitate the management, organization, monitoring, and evaluation of marketing campaigns, with a focus on comparing campaign performance, results, and budgets.
